# Sourced creative principles
Research basis: campaign text read; videos were not watched and frames were not inspected. These are lessons, not permission to reproduce campaigns or promises of results.

- Slack / Sandwich (https://sandwich.co/clients/slack/): scepticism and adoption became a documentary-style story. Show a human situation changed by actual product behaviour, rather than listing features.
- Mailchimp / Droga5 (https://droga5.com/work/mailchimp/): nine rhyming mini-campaigns formed a curiosity mechanism. Generate different mechanisms and plan brand payoff. Delayed branding is not a default short-ad rule.
- IKEA / Mother / Effie (https://effie.org/insights/ikea-mother-london-the-wonderful-everyday/): surreal domestic films elevated one lived category benefit through the brand. Focus on a benefit the product causes, not every feature.
- KFC / Mother (https://www.motherlondon.com/work/kfc-believe): cultural uncertainty anchored a playful product platform. Connect audience tension with a distinctive product truth, not random spectacle.
- 7 Brew / TikTok (https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/inspiration/7-brew-case-study): UGC, nostalgic and behind-the-scenes styles explored different entry points rather than recolouring a montage.
- Apartments.com / TikTok (https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/inspiration/apartments-dot-com-video-shopping-ads-case-study): relevant property catalogue ads served retargeting intent. Match storytelling to purchase stage.
- British Airways / One Club (https://www.oneclub.org/awards/theoneshow/-award/52139/a-british-original/): individual travel reasons supported varied writing/contextual art direction, including type moving with sail material. Sparse assets can support a strong written/visual idea; motion embodies meaning.
- Little Moons / Attest (https://www.askattest.com/customers/little-moons): habitual premium purchasers differed from social followers. Judge for purchasing audience, not trend engagement.
- TikTok property guidance (https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/guides/real-estate-advertising-guide): lifestyle story, actual room/neighbourhood evidence, clear copy and relevant action. Never generate missing actual rooms as evidence.

Operational synthesis: audience/occasion/objective, insight, supported argument, exact opening promise, story mechanism, composition, typography, motion, imagery, sound, pacing and ending. Tie each choice to this brief. Rich footage merits selection and continuity; sparse assets merit deliberate typography, diagrams, authorised stills or screenshots. Never invent evidence.

Sector possibilities, not templates: software can demonstrate real workflow or human observation; ecommerce can reveal actual use, sensory detail or verified comparison; restaurants can build appetite around real food and occasion; property can orient through actual layout and supported lifestyle. No universal sequence/end card.

Retain conceptual lessons, not wording, music, footage, signature scenes or identity. Agency/platform outcomes are contextual, not independently verified predictions.
