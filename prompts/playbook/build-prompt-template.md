# Bespoke creative build brief

Use the ordered brand, format and exact duration. New composition; preserve other assets.

## What makes this different
Audience, occasion, objective, insight and supported argument. Explain difference from recent concepts across hook, story mechanism, composition, motion, imagery, pacing and ending.

## Story and visual system
Opening promise, development, evidence and resolution in a few sentences. Choose sequence for this brief: continuous shot, editorial sequence, demonstration, metaphor, sensory film or observational story can work. No compulsory hook/problem/UI/proof/CTA sequence.
Describe composition, type hierarchy, motion purpose, imagery, pacing and sound. Interface only when proving actual product behaviour. Sparse assets require intentional design, never invented footage/evidence.

## Scene by scene
Necessary scenes only, each with exact start/end, on-screen copy, action, supplied files and sound. Cover the full duration; allow time to understand. One continuous scene is permitted when justified. A beat is not automatically a scene.
Specify focal subject, crop, negative space and type. No compulsory chips, counters, device frames, parallax or canned movement distances.

## Evidence and demonstration data
Factual sources and limitations. Approved real evidence; masked illustrative data identified when needed to avoid deception. No fictional testimonials/results portrayed as real.

## Voice and audio
Timed narration text/windows around 2 words per second, actual measured duration. Pauses, ambient sound/music and silence serve story. Caption actual speech only; identify rights and unsupported requests.

## Ending
Resolve opening promise, one suitable next step, final image and readable duration. CTA button, end card and fixed hold are optional.

## Guardrails and acceptance
Supplied brand tokens, safe zones, caption reservation, readable text, media treatments, exact paths, deterministic self-contained renderer. Exact ordered dimensions/duration at 30fps. Review meaningful moments.
Report plan, evidence, conflicts and voice fit first. Separate technical compliance from creative critique. Missing/failed review is unresolved, never approval. Bounded repairs must surface remaining issues. Do not claim watched/listened inspection unless it occurred.
